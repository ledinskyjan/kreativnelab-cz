The short version

  • Keep clear space of at least the height of the letter k around the logo.
  • Minimum size: logotype 30 mm or 120 px wide, the kl mark 10 mm or 32 px.
  • Don’t recolour, rotate or distort it, and don’t add frames or shadows.
  • Do not straighten the tilted letters: the wobble is deliberate.
  • Only place it on photos where the background is calm and there’s enough contrast. Otherwise, use the inverse version.
